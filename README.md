# Auto Repair Business Dashboard – Aslam Repairing Works

## Overview
Business performance dashboard analysing 6 years of billing and inventory 
data (2014–2020) for a commercial vehicle repair workshop in Kota, Rajasthan.

## Tools Used
Microsoft Excel — Pivot Tables, Charts, Conditional Formatting, Dynamic Filters

## Key Insights
- Monthly revenue trends across 6 years
- Payment collection rate tracking
- Top revenue-generating vehicle types and repair categories  
- Pending dues tracker with overdue flagging

## Skills Demonstrated
Advanced Excel | Dashboard Development | Business Analysis | KPI Reporting
